🪞 Reflection

I had the opportunity to attend an industry talk by Dr. Zahari Othman, where I gained valuable insights into critical thinking, creative thinking and Higher Order Thinking Skills (HOTS).

One important lesson I learned is that learning is not just about memorising information but also about understanding, analysing and solving problems independently.

I also realised that creative thinking plays an important role in cloud computing, as it helps develop innovative solutions such as cloud storage and automatic backup systems. Overall, this talk was a meaningful experience that encouraged me to think creatively, improve my problem-solving skills and continue learning.
